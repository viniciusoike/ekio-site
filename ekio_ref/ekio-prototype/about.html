Vinicius Oike Reginatto

Founder & Principal Consultant

With a Master's degree in Economics from the University of São Paulo (USP) and over 8 years of experience in business consulting, I founded EKIO to bridge a gap I saw repeatedly in the Brazilian market: the disconnect between rigorous economic analysis, modern data science, and spatial intelligence.

Most consulting firms excel in one discipline but struggle to integrate across domains. EKIO was built from the ground up to operate at these intersections — combining econometric modeling with machine learning, overlaying spatial analysis on economic forecasting, and translating complex data into clear, actionable insights for decision-makers.

My approach is rooted in the belief that data-driven decisions are better decisions, but only when the underlying methodology is sound. Every project at EKIO starts with understanding the problem deeply before reaching for tools.

How we work

Every engagement follows a structured methodology designed to deliver clarity and rigor, regardless of project complexity.

01 UNDERSTAND

Deep dive into your business context, data landscape, and decision-making needs. We ask hard questions before writing a single line of code.

02 ANALYZE

Rigorous methodology selection and execution. Econometrics, ML, spatial analysis — the right tool for the right question, never the reverse.

03 COMMUNICATE

Results translated into clear narratives with data visualizations designed for your audience — whether it's a board room or a municipal planning team.

04 IMPLEMENT

Actionable deliverables — not shelf-ware reports. We provide reproducible code, interactive tools, and ongoing support to ensure impact.
